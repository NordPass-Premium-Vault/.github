## Overview

NordPass Premium Vault is a password management concept built around encrypted credential storage and secure sharing. It brings together breach monitoring, password health checks, and cross-device access into a single environment for individuals and families.

---

## Essential NordPass Premium Features

- **Encrypted Vault:** Store passwords, passkeys, credit cards, and secure notes in an xChaCha20-encrypted vault .
- **Password Sharing:** Share credentials securely with family members or trusted contacts .
- **Password Health:** Identify weak, reused, and old passwords through the built-in health dashboard [citation:3].
- **Data Breach Scanner:** Monitor email addresses and credit cards for exposure in known data breaches [citation:3][citation:8].
- **Email Masking:** Generate unlimited email aliases for signups without exposing your real address [citation:8][citation:12].
- **Cross-Device Access:** Stay logged in across devices without re-entering credentials each time .

## What NordPass Premium Brings to Credential Management

The platform provides a structured environment for managing sensitive credentials. Its design supports both everyday logins and broader account security practices.

Encrypted storage forms the foundation. The vault uses xChaCha20 encryption, and the service operates on a zero-knowledge architecture, meaning NordPass cannot access stored data [citation:8][citation:10].

Password sharing extends the vault beyond a single user. Premium subscribers can share credentials with family members or trusted contacts, with action-based permissions such as Can Edit, Can Autofill, Can Share, and Can View .

Security monitoring adds a proactive layer. Password Health flags weak or reused passwords, while the Data Breach Scanner checks whether email addresses or credit card numbers appear in known breach reports [citation:3][citation:8].

Email masking reduces exposure during signups. Users can generate unlimited aliases and store them in the vault, limiting the use of a primary email address across services [citation:8][citation:12].

Cross-device convenience keeps the vault accessible. Premium allows switching between devices without repeated logins, and the service is available on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, and major browsers [citation:3].

Family coverage extends the concept to multiple users. The Family plan includes six separate encrypted vaults with access to all Premium features, allowing one owner to share five additional vaults with family or friends .
